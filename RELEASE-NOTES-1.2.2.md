# EMRALD v1.2.2 — Release Notes

A small quality-of-life release: minor fixes and a couple of quiet additions.

## Added

- **Proactive burnout warnings** — when your patterns show sustained strain, EMRALD now says so in the sidebar instead of waiting for you to open the Burnout Monitor. It's deliberately restrained: snoozeable for three days, and capped at two notices per episode so it never nags.
- **Check-in streak** — a small counter in the sidebar showing consecutive days with an energy check-in.

## Fixed

- Hour-override modal copy now accurately describes how the adjustment applies to your schedule
- Effort-profile modal sliders render full-width, matching the other modals

No breaking changes. No settings changes. `minAppVersion` stays at 1.7.2.
