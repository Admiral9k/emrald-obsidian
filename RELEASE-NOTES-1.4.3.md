# EMRALD 1.4.3

A shorter first run.

Improvements:

- Setup is now Connect, Welcome, your first project, and Ready, where you pick your hours and your first session starts.
- The profile questions are optional and sit outside setup. Open them from Effort Profile or the command "Fill in your profile". Nothing is pre-answered, and a skipped question stays blank.
- The six work types have plain names.
- The first-receipt welcome appears once per account, in any vault.
- The Connect screen lists what syncs.

Fixes:

- When a save fails during setup, you see a message and your answers stay. Skip for now moves on without saving.
- An account whose projects cannot be checked when you connect is no longer sent through setup.
- Closing setup with Esc while it saves no longer opens About on top of your session.
- The "were you in the zone" question on the receipt starts blank.

`minAppVersion` stays at 1.13.0.
