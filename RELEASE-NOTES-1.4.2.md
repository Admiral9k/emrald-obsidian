# EMRALD 1.4.2

Plainer words.

Improvements:

- The plugin's wording now matches the web app, in everyday words: recaps instead of digests, Effort Allocations instead of E-Level Overview, and plain names for the metrics and your effort profile.
- Set your weekly recap day and hour in your own local time.
- The sidebar counts your check-ins this week in place of a streak.
- The Burnout Monitor says "Not enough data yet" when there is no score to judge from.
- Warnings and notices are shorter and easier to read.

Fixes:

- The plugin no longer overwrites a recap schedule you changed on the web.

`minAppVersion` stays at 1.13.0.
